**Ideas**

- AI-generated quiz b4 collect badge; said badge/collectible is Manifest asset?
- Simulate a Manifest API for teachers to create content; will immediately load our premade content, “demo”
- Involve real-world problems & communities
    - Ex: quest asking to email mayor to fix lights issue
    - Ex: quest asking collaboration b/w Indonesian & English student
    - Ex: co-create quests with communities, local businesses; e.g. have quests where must talk to museum staff
- Rewards involve real-world experiences
    - Ex: if get enough badges, can get free experience e.g. watercolor painting class
- Workflow designer for teacher to interleave e.g. field trips to get knowledge & badges (e.g. ppl can go to museums in pairs), field trips to apply skills (e.g. whole group goes watercolor painting class); LLM interface w/ human-in-loop editing

**Example Workflow**

- Experience creator: creates content for experiences, uploads info to be put in quiz, creates assets for different possible objects/sites
    - Multiple stages in interface, e.g. rules; LLM helps to generate group experiences, etc.
- Student: go to museum, visit specific object/sites, learn about it, take quiz/photo (depending on teacher’s intent), get badge/collectible
    - Take photo of object/have camera look at it, used as “key” to dictionary of existing assets that you will get
    - Types of badges: discovery (take picture of thing, e.g. statue), knowledge (complete quiz)
        - Discovery: collectible is model of thing
        - Knowledge: collectible is model of entire site
        - Easter eggs, e.g. at Hawking’s “look at sky, not just ground”, if student lifts camera up, get easter egg badge; for when they go beyond teacher’s instructions
    - Students can get grouped up, each have specific role, quests on app; to get badge must work together (like driver-navigator game)
        - Types of quests: as above, e.g. discovery, knowledge, easter eggs
        - Say one group has Role A and Role B; if Role A completes quest, generates QR scanner; if Role B completes quest, generates QR code; must combine to unlock special badge
        - Easter eggs: shared by the whole team
    - Then get recommender system’s (OR LLM-generated) rec on where to go next, e.g. if see flowers in X place, go to Botanic Garden
